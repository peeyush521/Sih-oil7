The Problem

India loses 3 workers every hour to industrial accidents.

Current Reality

  • Incident reports sit in filing cabinets
  • Each report is investigated in isolation
  • Patterns across time are invisible
  • Safety officers review 100+ reports manually
  • By the time a pattern is noticed, someone is already hurt

Oil India Specific

  • 1,100+ km of pipeline network
  • Multiple well pads, processing units, gas plants
  • Reports in English and Hindi mixed
  • Field workers write messy shorthand notes
  • No system connects incidents across time
"We don't have a data problem. We have a connection problem. Every incident has clues — we just can't see them."

How It Works

5 AI Layers

  • spaCy NER — 230 Oil India domain terms
  • Sentence Transformers — 384-dim semantic embeddings
  • TF-IDF + Logistic Regression — 7-class incident classifier
  • NetworkX Knowledge Graph — Temporal incident linking
  • Google Gemini — Natural language explanations

9-Factor Risk Engine

  • Frequency of related incidents
  • Recency (time since last incident)
  • Severity trend (escalating?)
  • Semantic similarity between reports
  • Equipment recurrence
  • Location clustering
  • Unresolved corrective actions
  • SIF pathway detection
  • Urgency score from text
